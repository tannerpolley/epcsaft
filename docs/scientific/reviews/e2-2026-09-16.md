## Changed behavior

- Support intent: an omitted support is completed; a declared support never gains
  an index; a declared empty support is rejected by name. Reaction participation
  is sign independent (nonzero stoichiometry), so reversing a reaction leaves
  support, rank and rows unchanged.
- Pins: feed-relative fractions are validated individually in `[0, 1]`; when the
  declared reaction space conserves total moles (`rank([B; e]) == rank(B)`), an
  over-sum and an all-pinned under-sum are exact input rejections. Nonconserving
  reaction spaces receive no total-mole sum rule. The blanket all-pinned rejection
  is removed; a square rank-deficient declaration returns the exact pre-solve
  `NOT_RUN`/`Stop::infeasible` record before any start or optimizer work.
- Reaction targets: exactly one of scalar `K`, affinity or the P3
  `ReactionLogPolynomial` on the EOS density basis (`eos-helmholtz-coordinate-basis`;
  any other identity is `reference-basis-mismatch`). The P3 owner now supplies
  second and third `ln K` temperature derivatives; the standard state carries
  exact value, first and second temperature terms and the former affine
  `s'' = -2 s'/T` shortcut is retired. A supplied Gibbs vector is checked against
  the targets by the inferred-vector criterion, and a free temperature with a
  constant supplied vector requires constant targets. Supplied species
  thermochemistry is compared with each target through `[t, T t', T^2 t'']` against
  `[g, g - h, -T cp]` on the density basis (`reference inconsistency`).
- Caller criterion: `EquilibriumProblem.row_tolerance` (default the named policy) sets
  the Ipopt tolerances and the row classification; a caller-valued criterion is
  owned by the convergence record. `requested_tolerance_met` is exactly
  `recomputed_residual <= row_tolerance` whenever a finite residual exists.
- Driver: one prepared phase point per distinct callback point shared by rows,
  Jacobian, gradient and curvature; weighted constraint curvature is recombined
  when the optimizer multipliers change; final certification stays fresh. The
  single declared pure phase no longer requires two stable density roots.
- Fixtures: `ascani_reactive_*` are renamed `synthetic_reactive_*` with `K = 1`,
  the relation their supplied `g = 0` actually evaluated; the E1 pinned-reactive
  case migrates to affinity-only 850 J/mol.

## Demonstrated limitation (open owner decision)

The correlated identical-species isomerisation (`ln K = 0.3 + 1200/T + 0.4 ln T
+ 0.002 T`, `K ~ 870-1010`, `x_A ~ 1e-3`) is an unpinned single-phase Gibbs
minimization. Its chemical rows are not Ipopt constraints; they are recovered from
the objective gradient through the least-squares balance multiplier, which
splits the affinity defect equally over both chemical rows, and the gradient of
the dilute log-composition coordinate is `N x_i mu_i`, so the defect is bounded
only by tolerance/(N x_A). Ipopt reports
`SUCCESS`, but the recomputed
row of the dilute species is `4.3e-9`, `3.7e-9` and `3.3e-9` at 330, 350 and 370 K
against the 1e-10 criterion, so the accepted classification returns `stalled` and
clears the state. Requested tolerances 1e-12 and 1e-13 give rows 6.9e-11 and 4.3e-12
that still miss their own request (the ratio row/tolerance is about 40 here and
grows like `1/x_i`); scalar `K = 2` converges at 4.8e-12 while `K = 50` already
reaches 1.9e-10. No association floor exists for this neutral pair. The composition is not retained
(the stalled state is cleared); for identical species the affinity defect `r`
bounds the composition defect by `x_A x_B r`, about 4e-12, and the installed-wheel
probe at scalar `K = 2` measured `|x_B - K/(1+K)| = 2.1e-12`. This is a
conditioning property of the accepted unpinned formulation, not a defect in the
correlation connection: the same correlation owner drives the passing A=2B case.
The retained rows are recorded as failures; the driver, criterion and fixture were
not altered. Candidate corrections for the owner are listed in the issue.

## Non-claims

No phase discovery, global stability, PH/PS/UV, continuation or solved-state
action is claimed. The manufactured reactive cases are numerical evidence, not
literature validation. The A=2B, charged and isomerisation cases use identical or
synthetic species; they test the compiler, standard state and driver composition,
not EOS accuracy.
