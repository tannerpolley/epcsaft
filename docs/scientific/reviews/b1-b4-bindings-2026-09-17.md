## Limits

- Transport agreement on manufactured fixtures through one build. It validates no MEA,
  reactive-LLE, HDA or electrolyte model, reference conversion or application.
- The 1e-12 absolute floor of the transport rule bounds relative resolution for small
  rows (3.7e-4 of the logistic A3, 8.8e-7 of the E5-10 third-order sum); the observed
  differences are zero and 5e-14, and the independent bounds above carry those claims.
- `requested_tolerance_met` is independent of `success`. In the exercised 1e-18 case
  (Ipopt tiny-step stop, `converged_to_floor`) the native owner rejects the point: finite
  row residuals, the achieved residual and the stop remain, and the native result clears
  its state vectors, so no candidate T, P, amounts or coordinates exist to transport. By
  source (`gibbs_minimizer.cpp`, accepted floor-limited branch) an Ipopt-successful
  floor-limited point keeps its state with `success = True` and the flag False; no probed
  tolerance reached that branch, so it is transported but not numerically exercised.
- B3c shows separate producers, lengths, row order and sign. The unit feed gives unit
  moiety scale, so the material-balance duals equal minus the balance coordinates here
  (to 2e-12); a constraint scale different from one is not exercised.
- Rows of magnitude below 1e-9 (root residual, floor residual, normalization duals, the
  mixed P/T sums) are compared relatively with no absolute floor. 301 of the 410 retained
  rows are consumed by a Python comparison; the rest are retained native context.
- `support_change` and `ionic_insertion` remain unforced native statuses (E5-8).
- A nested record read from Python is a copy: replace `component_intervals`, do not
  mutate an element in place. Scalar K stays constant in T; condensed-source conversion,
  full chemical potentials, HDA pressure-chart ln(phi_i) outputs, ASL transport and
  regression are outside this pass.
- #74: the four focused `test_state.py` checks pass on the final wheel. The historical v5
  Khudaida campaign still stops at its missing `ionic_dispersion` declaration (#75); no
  campaign was replayed.
