## Changed behavior

- One continuation controller, `continue_equilibrium(model, request)`, follows a straight
  dimensionless path `q` in fixed intensives and resolved feed amounts between two
  problems with identical declarations. The predictor uses the existing implicit
  tangent (`lever_jacobian` and `sensitivity`); the corrector is the same Ipopt driver
  seeded through the private full-coordinate route `solve_equilibrium_from_coordinates`.
- Acceptance of a path state requires driver acceptance, `requested_tolerance_met` at the
  caller `row_tolerance`, no candidate-topology event, and each corrected phase density
  inside the anchored locally unique window (`DENSITY_ANCHOR_LOG_WINDOW`) of its cold
  declared root at `q = 0` or of the previous accepted density afterwards, with a
  positive corrected pressure slope. The corrected composition, density, temperature and
  pressure are recomputed from the coordinates through `prepare`, never read from a
  supplied result's arrays.
- Passage through a critical point between consecutive accepted states, where every
  nonzero component of a pair's inter-phase difference vector (Δln η and each Δx_i)
  changes sign, is that pair's coalescence: terminal `phases_coalesced` with the pair
  named and the swapped stationary state retained as `last_result` (owner decision
  2026-09-16, after the E4-D finding below). The phase-distance predicate alone cannot
  see a crossing that falls between two steps.
- `EquilibriumResult` carries the producer's typed `topology_event`, `topology_phase` and
  `topology_pair` (first vanished free phase in declaration order, else the first
  coincident pair); `check_topology` is the one predicate shared by the driver and the
  controller's initial admission.
- Retry: left-domain, stalled and missing-anchor failures halve the step from the last
  accepted coordinates up to the retry allowance and minimum step; invalid paths,
  nonfinite operands, infeasible solves, a singular tangent at the accepted state, a
  generated-point rank loss (`singular_jacobian`), typed disappearance/coalescence, the
  swap above and a corrected density outside the branch window are terminal. A
  predicted free amount below zero is projected onto its bound so the bound-constrained
  corrector reports the disappearance event; the projection count is retained per
  attempt (`predictor_projected`). The driver bounds and the predictor domain share
  `PACKING_FRACTION_FLOOR`.
- Owner decisions applied during this slice (recorded in #41 and #39): the Ipopt inner
  tolerances are `min(row_tolerance, EQUILIBRIUM_ROW_RESIDUAL) ×
  EQUILIBRIUM_INNER_TOLERANCE_FACTOR` while the caller criterion classifies; free-amount
  cells classify the KKT-scaled stationarity residuals `N_p x_i μ̃_i / N_feed` and
  `N_p / N_feed × pressure row` with multipliers recovered by the KKT-weighted fit, and
  retain the unscaled rows as diagnostics; a singular tangent is terminal; a pair swap
  between consecutive accepted states is that pair's coalescence. The earlier decision
  that an anchored-root loss persisting at `minimum_step` is an accepted admission-limit
  end still stands as a rule, but it was recorded on a false premise for E4-D (the
  loss occurred 4 K past an undetected critical crossing) and no retained path ends
  that way now.

## Correction history

The first evidence set (`f4fc22ed`, at `a5c751d1`) reported E4-D as an anchored-root
loss at 270.0 K "near the mixture critical point". The Numerical Execution review
reproduced the path on the installed wheel and showed that the density difference and
the vapor composition change sign at 266.0 K with a phase distance of 2.7e-3 (above the
1e-6 threshold), that the 58 later states carry the declared liquid lighter than the
declared vapor (the 50/50 mixture's dew-side branch with the labels swapped), and that
the 270 K loss is the temperature-parametrised fold of that branch. The supporting
cold-solve inference was invalid: cold bubble seeds collapse from 240 K, where the
continuation has distinct phases. The swap predicate above is the owner's response; the
E4-D rows and this text were regenerated from the corrected binaries.

## Limits

The free-amount classification certifies stationarity in the solved (ln x, ln eta)
chart: a dilute species' or vanishing phase's intensive potential row is certified only
to tolerance × N_feed / (N_p x_i); the amount-stationarity (Gibbs-dual) component is not a
classified row and is bounded only by the optimizer's inner dual tolerance (S1's
Gibbs-dual rows check it independently, 4.0e-12 here); the inner-tolerance factor is a
provisional heuristic covering the measured 1.0002–1.02× overshoot, not an error bound.
Continuation acceptance enforces density continuity (the 1 % window per step) and the
row/topology gates; composition continuity is inferred, not enforced. E4-C's event
brackets the true disappearance within one step.

The swap predicate detects a crossing only when it falls between two accepted states
whose difference vectors are both informative; it locates the critical point within one
step (265.9–266.0 K here) and does not solve the critical-point equations. A step large
enough to leave the anchor window is rejected before the predicate runs, so a
crossing can also surface as `branch_lost`. No test produces `budget_exhausted`,
`left_domain`, `initial_failure` or a `singular_jacobian` at a generated point: no
fixture in this program reaches those paths. The continuation result is a declared local
branch, not discovery, global stability or an EOS-accuracy claim; a 0.5 K bubble step
loses the incipient vapor anchor (the 1 % window bounds the admissible step). No
PH/PS/UV, solids or solved-state action is claimed.
