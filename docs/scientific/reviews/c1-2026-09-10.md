## Method and correction

The centered polynomial retains its accepted basis and reference anchors.
`ALG-CALORICS` in `engine/docs/science/algorithms.md` owns the
representation and error argument: exact constant-Cp logarithm; a positive
transformed series below `u=1`; normalized recurrence above it; and a negative
series/logarithmic-tail crossover derived from degree. Coefficients multiply
powers from the start, avoiding overflow of an unweighted power. Inactive
branches have finite arguments. State-dependent choices remain on the AD tape;
loop lengths depend only on immutable degree and floating-point precision.

The previous fixed negative crossover could subtract an exponentially small
remainder at high degree. The final crossover bounds that cancellation, and the
negative series length follows a geometric remainder bound. These are arithmetic
representations of the same accepted integral, not new thermodynamic inputs.

Source interval ownership, source pressure, NASA constants, the unmodified
Shomate increment/formation enthalpy, and even DIPPR anchored limits remain
explicit. The two equilibrium mechanical-energy sums now use one Contributions
owner with their original clamp choices and off-normalized chart. The residual
Helmholtz tape is unchanged. Evidence input strings use the existing serializer's
field delimiter so separate coefficients and anchors survive JSON conversion.
