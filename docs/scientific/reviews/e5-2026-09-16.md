## Changed behavior

- One native owner, `solved_state_actions(compiled, central, request)`, returns exact
  first-, second- and third-order mixed directional actions of physical solved-state
  observables (T, P, phase amount, component amounts, mole fractions, extensive volume,
  molar density, packing fraction, phase pressure, residual chemical potential over RT
  and the fifteen P4 calorics) with respect to physical-unit controls (T, P, resolved
  feed amounts, active parameters). It performs no nonlinear solve, no compilation and
  no mutation; raw `sensitivity` stays an internal coordinate helper.
- Each mixed action is the half-sign polarisation of diagonal control paths; along a
  path the state coefficients follow J y_k = −[t^k] F(y(t), q(t)) on one factorisation
  of the row Jacobian. The row owner's new `row_series` composes a CppAD geometry map
  of the analytic row terms (log chart, packing volume, standard state to third order
  in the driven or free temperature, balance, charge, normalisation) with the model's
  residual gradient coefficients; the Properties owner's `residual_energy_action`
  replaces `third_directional` in place and supplies the E3 and E4 contractions with
  typed P4 statuses and diagnostics. The E1 pinned-Hessian caller migrates to it; the
  Properties runtime stays at 3434 lines (net zero).
- Observables are mapped along the same curved path; phase calorics compose P4
  density-chart actions of each order with the lower-order curvature terms.
- Owner decisions applied (2026-09-16, recorded in #42): the admission gate is the
  classified residual the driver certifies (the unscaled rows are retained in the
  evidence); the reference ladders compare against the h²-extrapolated centered-solve
  reference and certify second-order convergence; the associating case uses physical
  directions because the scaled A3 corners left the feed domain.
- Review corrections (`54378fd7`): a central-state gate overrides every state-dependent
  row's status ahead of the selected P4 dependency; a zero direction is admitted before
  the thermal-reference gate; the P4 composition directions have their roundoff along
  (1, z) removed before the trust boundary; a rounding-bounded ladder (finest difference
  growing again) extrapolates from its two coarser steps; every ladder row retains its
  best-step relative agreement and whether its reference sits below the absolute floor;
  case 6 verifies each corner's declared-branch root; A→2B composition and amount
  references use E2's pure-state relation at each corner temperature; case 4 checks
  direction-order symmetry; the matrix owner's rank-deficient row is retained.

## Limits

The claim is local to the declared topology, support, coordinate chart and smooth
branch; it is not a stability, discovery or global-equilibrium certificate. Admission
certifies the classified residual (decision 2); the unscaled chemical rows of a dilute
participant remain bounded only by tolerance × N_feed / (N_p x_i) and are retained per
row. The reference ladders certify second-order convergence in place of the original
two-times-criterion refinement rule where the steps are informative; 188 of 411 rows sit
below the absolute floor, where the criterion alone would admit a zero action, and 55
ladders are rounding-bounded at the finest step (per-corner solve noise of about 1e-15
relative on calorics of 5e4 J/mol), so the falsifying strength of those rows is the
retained best-step relative agreement, not the criterion. The mutation checks the body
lists (sign flip, dropped contraction, lowered energy order) are inferred from the
ladder tolerances, not retained as runs. Unforced typed statuses, retained as claim
limits: `support_change` and `ionic_insertion` (no admitted central state carries a
removed species), `phase_boundary_not_differentiable` and
`nonunique_coalesced_phases` (no successful result carries a topology event),
`rank_deficient` in the action owner (the matrix owner's rank row is retained),
`energy_order_unavailable` (the model supplies E3/E4 and P4 the caloric orders),
`density_unavailable`, `pressure_singular`, the association statuses and
`central_property_unavailable` (P4 dependencies not forced through E5). The
`nonfinite_jacobian`, `linear_solve_residual` and `nonfinite` paths are unexecuted, not
merely unforced. The shared tape is re-recorded when a residual contraction and an
ideal-bearing property chain alternate (performance only); the migrated E1 caller
throws `runtime_error` where the removed method threw `invalid_argument` (no catch site
depends on the class). A domain_error from a zero leading jet coefficient remains the
native boundary. No public mapping, no reacting heat capacity and no application
aggregate is claimed.
