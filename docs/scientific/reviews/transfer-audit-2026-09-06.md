### F2 — exact-zero associating species can make valid support boundaries unevaluable

**Verified, priority 1.** [site_factorization](/home/tnnrpolley21/Workspaces/Engineering/ePC-SAFT-greenfield/engine/native/model/helmholtz_model.cpp:57) multiplies association curvature rows by component amount/site multiplicity. At zero amount for an associating component, a weighted row vanishes. Full state and pressure-slope requests then encounter singular factorization, although the underlying unweighted mass-action equations may remain regular.

For the full nine-species MEA revision-3 topology at T=313.15 K, ρ=30,000 mol/m³, pure-water and pure-CO₂ vectors each returned `EosError: association Hessian is singular`. A vector with water=.8, MEA=.1, CO₂=.05, protonated MEA=.02, carbamate=.02, bicarbonate=.005, hydronium=.005 and zero carbonate/hydroxide evaluated. Its negative pressure is a mathematical fixed-density result, not a physical equilibrium acceptance. This finding concerns absent **associating** components retained in a full topology; it does not establish failure of pure nonassociating ethane saturation.

A later isolating probe kept positive ionic content: at T=313.15 K, ρ=10000 mol/m³, x=(0,.9,.05,.02,.02,.005,0,.005,0) failed with the same association error; replacing water/MEA by (.01,.89) evaluated, with P=−118866371.85326037 Pa. Both are normalized and electroneutral; negative pressure limits the claim to mathematical fixed-density evaluation. The [support-probe rows](/home/tnnrpolley21/Workspaces/Engineering/ePC-SAFT-greenfield/docs/scientific/reviews/transfer-audit-2026-09-06-support.csv) retain all seven probes, including two exploratory controls that remained singular with other associating species absent.

**Reference-backed limit qualification:** the DH leading term scales as ionic strength to the 3/2 power, so second insertion derivatives can diverge at zero ionic strength even when the residual value and first derivative have a limit. Pure-water/CO₂ probes alone therefore do not prove that a full arbitrary-direction Hessian should exist. Separate this genuine lack of smoothness from the weighted association singularity; supported value/T/ρ or within-support derivatives may remain available while ionic insertion Hessians are unavailable.

**Inference:** use the mass-action implicit Jacobian or a mathematically equivalent support-aware elimination while preserving one-sided insertion limits. Do not turn structural zeros into arbitrary positive mole fractions. Ordinary derivatives can still be unavailable at genuine phase/support transitions; report that distinction.

### F5 — advertised exact Ipopt Hessians do not satisfy the callback contract

**Verified source defect; effect on particular solves not measured.** [CompiledSystem::hessian](/home/tnnrpolley21/Workspaces/Engineering/ePC-SAFT-greenfield/engine/native/equilibrium/request_compile.cpp:822) returns a zero matrix whenever any phase amount is pinned. The pinned formulation has a zero objective but nonlinear stationarity/pressure/normalization constraints. [solve_equilibrium](/home/tnnrpolley21/Workspaces/Engineering/ePC-SAFT-greenfield/engine/native/equilibrium/gibbs_minimizer.cpp:548) selects `hessian_approximation=exact` unconditionally. Zero objective curvature does not make arbitrary multiplier-weighted nonlinear constraint curvature zero. This includes the free-T/P boundary cases: compilation enforces `free_intensive_count == pinned_count`, so currently admitted free intensives take this zero-Hessian early return. The remaining finite-phase Hessian body reads fixed `problem.temperature.value/pressure.value`; those reads are not a separately reachable free-intensive defect under that compiler invariant. Any future exact pinned Hessian must read solved intensive coordinates and populate their mixed curvature, not reuse the fixed-intensive body unchanged.

In the finite-phase case, [eval_h](/home/tnnrpolley21/Workspaces/Engineering/ePC-SAFT-greenfield/engine/native/equilibrium/gibbs_minimizer.cpp:472) multiplies the entire returned objective-plus-constraint Hessian by `obj_factor`. The required matrix is `obj_factor*H(f) + Σ λᵢ H(gᵢ)`, so constraint terms must not receive that factor. The error is hidden at `obj_factor=1`; other factors, including zero, expose it. This contract is explicit in the [Ipopt Hessian example](https://coin-or.github.io/Ipopt/INTERFACES.html), which scales objective curvature then separately adds constraint curvature.

**Recommendation:** correct weighting at the shared callback. For pinned equality problems, choose an honest numerical method: a root method using the existing exact Jacobian, a qualified limited-memory constrained solve, or the genuinely exact weighted constraint Hessian. The last option can require third Helmholtz derivatives for μ/p constraints. Select by retained boundary accuracy and measured cost; do not advertise zero curvature as exact. If a limited-memory method is selected, the same slice must revise the current exact-only check in `tools/check-engine-architecture.py` and the unconditional `GibbsMinimizer::uses_exact_hessian()` claim to describe the actual mathematical method. Preserve exact Jacobian requirements and numerical reference checks; do not merely remove enforcement. This does not show that every reported boundary root is wrong—final residual checks can still identify a root—but convergence/efficiency and exact-derivative claims are unsupported until qualified.

Add a native numerical falsifier: compare the callback Hessian action against a centered directional difference of `obj_factor*∇f + Jgᵀλ`, at fixed nonzero λ and `obj_factor` 0, .5 and 1, for both declared finite and pinned cases. Use a step ladder and a regular point, including an analytic exponential normalization row whose curvature is nonzero. These λ are Ipopt callback constraint multipliers, distinct from chemical multiplier coordinates embedded in the pinned primal vector. Exercise the full pinned vector, free T/P away from seed values, and cross-curvature with those embedded coordinates; reconstruct the symmetric Hessian from the callback lower triangle before comparing its action. No private shape/name assertion can substitute for this check.

### Derivative orders follow the observable

Let Φ(T,V,n,θ)=Aʳ/(RT), and let y(q) solve F(y,q)=0 on a fixed branch and support. Association sites are implicit variables, not constants simply because the tape receives their values dynamically. **Verified:** current tape records independent T,V,n and six active families, with dynamic site fractions and explicit first/second stationary correction. That is a legitimate reduced derivative method within its nonsingular domain. It does not automatically supply third/fourth reduced derivatives or fit sensitivities for coefficients still stored as fixed doubles. [CppAD independent/dynamic semantics](https://cppad.readthedocs.io/latest/Independent.html) support this distinction.

| Requested quantity | Generic derivative order of underlying Helmholtz energy | Required qualification |
|---|---:|---|
| p, μ, residual h/u values | 1 | Correct T factors, units and reference convention |
| Their first derivatives; fixed-composition cv/cp values | 2 | cp also uses fixed-P density response; Pρ≠0 |
| cp/cv sensitivities in T, composition or θ | 3 | Includes mixed derivatives and implicit density chain rule |
| First equilibrium map derivative y_q when F contains μ or p | 2 | Full-rank F_y / regular active KKT system |
| Second equilibrium map action D²y | 3 | Derivatives of F_y and F_q; association reduced derivatives at that order |
| Third equilibrium map action D³y | 4, generically | Restricted smooth branch; thermal correlations differentiated consistently |
| Second total-equilibrium enthalpy action D²H | Usually 3 plus equilibrium chain terms | Declared reference cp slope; reaction/reference data and normalization |
| Exact Hessian of an outer residual that already uses D_q μ or D_qH | May require 4 through A3 equilibrium/property actions | Derive from the actual graph, not solver branding |

These orders are **reference-backed mathematical deductions**, not a demand for dense tensors. At first order `F_y y_q = −F_q`; for two directions a,b:

`F_y y_ab = −(F_ab + F_ay y_b + F_by y_a + F_yy[y_a,y_b])`.

For G(y(q),q), `D_qG = G_q + G_y y_q`; fixed-P density obeys `ρ_q|P = −P_q/P_ρ` for q other than P, and `ρ_P=1/P_ρ`. Differentiate the same equation for mixed actions. Rank loss, disappearance, branch switches and exhausted solves invalidate ordinary implicit derivatives; an empty array without a reason is inadequate. [Ceres' implicit-function treatment](https://ceres-solver.readthedocs.io/latest/inverse_and_implicit_function_theorems.html) explicitly relies on a locally invertible Jacobian. AD/analytic production and finite-difference validation play different roles, as described in [Ceres derivative methods](https://ceres-solver.readthedocs.io/latest/derivatives.html).

The current active tape has SegmentCount, SigmaConstant, DispersionEnergyOverK, Kij, AssociationEnergyOverK and AssociationVolume. Ionic diameter/Born/permittivity/suppression coefficients, nonconstant diameter coefficients, reference thermochemistry and K(T) coefficients cannot be declared “covered” by those six names. Extend the typed field-to-coefficient mapping only for admitted equations and actual fit directions, with fixed/shared parameters explicit. Dynamic AD parameters intentionally have no derivatives with respect to their values.

### Thermochemistry and regression must share a consistent basis

For ideal standard states, converting fixed source pressure P₀ to a fixed density reference ρ₀ adds `RT ln(RTρ₀/P₀)` to standard g. The corresponding standard h shift is `−RT`; for reaction Δν the derivative of ln K changes by `−Δν/T`. These are **reference-backed thermodynamic identities**, and the fixed source P₀ must not silently become the solved pressure. Reconstructing g° from ν and ln K determines only the reaction subspace; absolute caloric reference information still needs a declared gauge/data basis. Test gauge invariance along conserved quantities rather than pretending the inverse stoichiometric system has a unique absolute solution.

Finite-dose absorption heat is an explicitly extensive difference between inlet and equilibrated outlet enthalpies divided by the declared absorbed dose; it needs both states, feed/reference bases and their coupled parameter derivatives. It is not the differential heat or fixed-composition cp.

**Inference:** Sum, Product, Log and typed leaves are a sufficient small algebra for pCO₂, ratios/selectivity and finite-dose differences, using the planned Constant leaf and Product exponents (including −1 for a reciprocal). The algebra does not create missing property leaves or derivatives. A fixed coordinate is a valid constant factor; the planned `Coordinate(P)` name-based failure on a fixed-P target should be replaced by mathematically correct zero sensitivity, with no-information/rank diagnosis at fit level. Do not create application-specific fit branches.

Use one prediction evaluation per state/problem and θ, reuse it across residuals, and expose physical parameter J after scaling/transforms. A failed Ceres prediction must return failure, never a successful zero residual; Ceres cannot guarantee recovery from an invalid initial evaluation. Report conditioning, singular values/rank, parameter correlations and active bounds. Covariance based on `(JᵀWJ)⁻¹` is local and conditional on identifiability and error assumptions. Multiply by estimated residual variance only when that variance is being estimated; the planned R8 universal `σ²` multiplier is not correct for every known-uncertainty weighting convention. A pseudoinverse is not evidence that unidentifiable parameters have finite physical uncertainty.
