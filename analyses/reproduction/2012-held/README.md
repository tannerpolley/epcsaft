# Held 2012 alcohol/salt densities and activities

Current native reproduction of [Held et al., *Measuring and modeling alcohol/salt systems*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ces.2011.09.040), with the paper's solvent-specific parameters. The [notebook](notebook.qmd) reads retained values only. All direct measurements miss the adopted half-print-increment criteria; agreement with the sampled model curves varies by quantity. This is a calibration comparison and numerical verification, not independent physical validation. Delivered independent review remains with the parent issue owner.

From this checkout's prepared editable environment:

```bash
nice -n 10 env -u EPCSAFT_HSL_LIBRARY EPCSAFT_BUILD_JOBS=6 .venv/bin/python analyses/reproduction/2012-held/scripts/reproduce.py
```

`--compare-only` regenerates comparison tables without Engine calculations or changing execution identities. `--refresh-figures` preserves already executed direct-table and shared-point values while refreshing corrected figure declarations and source readings. Corrected source-only readings were frozen in their own commit before the delivered-review recalculation; the execution identity retains that commit and file-list hash.

The adopted packet is [Held 2012 alcohols /1](../../../data/packets/held-2012-alcohols/1/packet.toml), materialized byte-for-byte under `inputs/parameters/held-2012-alcohols/`. Source tables, masses, tolerances, source inconsistencies, figure ownership exclusions and fixed-composition declarations are retained under `inputs/`. The sole source-derived declaration producer is `scripts/prepare.py`; it needs the embedded JPEGs extracted from the identified PDF in `build/issue-735/` and is not part of an ordinary model rerun. It validates inputs through the packet command and never computes a thermodynamic prediction.

[Comparisons](results/comparisons.csv), [summary comparisons](results/summary-comparisons.csv), and [compressed native records](results/native-results.json.gz) retain the values and execution identities. Missing original cohorts or unidentified source row selections leave much of Tables 7–8 undetermined; the existing Esteso Table I remains its observation owner. The ethanol/LiI maximum-concentration contradiction remains unresolved. Figure 7 lacks its measured permittivities. Figure 5's shortest ethanol and low-water curves are not digitized: ambiguous ownership; crossings, marker overlays and merged dilute segments are excluded throughout. The complete series inventory remains in `inputs/figures/calibration.json`.

Fixed mixed-solvent scalar declarations support density and ionic activity only at the declared solvent ratio. They do not supply solvent-ratio derivatives or mixed-solvent solvent activities. Methanol is fixed at the declared temperature. The averaged ion size supplies packing and screening; transformed solvent–ion pair coordinates retain solvent-specific dispersion cross sizes and energies. All primary ion screening diameters are the printed sigma; packing uses the separately sourced factor. The [screening comparisons](results/screening-comparisons.csv) retain the actual Figure 2/3/5/6 activity states with unchanged packing and shorter screening. This probe improves water and ethanol solvent coefficients and ethanol osmotic coefficients strongly; methanol and several mixed curves remain discrepant. It does not establish Held's historical convention.

The [native pair-size check](scripts/check-pair-sizes.cpp) reads declarations emitted by `reproduce.py --pair-size-inputs`, admits them with the Engine parameter builder and compares native cross sizes with solvent-specific source sizes. Its [retained values](results/pair-sizes.csv) and build/run commands in `build/issue-735/pair-size-check.sh` cover every mixed declaration at the stated temperature, with a 1e-12 Å equality criterion. The notebook reports the two review witnesses and maximum difference.

The distinct Figiel /4 shared comparison reproduces the prior native density exactly and still misses Held's measured density. It keeps the historical masses, parameter hash and old/new execution identities separately from the Held packet. The retired subset templates and plots were replaced after the live record owned their source comparisons; Git preserves them, including the historical records cited in the accepted design.
