SI Table S11 (transcription lines 162–170) gives five mass O/A ratios, measured extraction percentages and paper-calculated percentages. At mass O/A 1.976, the two columns are 93.50% measured and 95.02% calculated. This row fitted the Li–TBP cross association; other ratios are prediction comparisons. Temperature is 294.15 K, pressure 1.013 bar, organic TBP mass fraction 0.85 and aqueous lithium mass fraction 0.002. Preserve this distinction from a 1 bar subsystem table. A source-model comparison would use 0.005 percentage point printed rounding; measured expanded uncertainty is stated as U=0.006 with k=2 and must have its fractional/percent convention checked in the PDF before scoring. No Engine extraction has been calculated here.

Article Table 3 and its note (transcription lines 93–118) set Debye–Hückel and Born ion diameters to 0.88 sigma and allow lithium cross association only with TBP. The frozen inputs' independent Born diameters 2.784 and 4.100 Angstrom must not substitute for this mapping. Tables 2–4 must be declared with their printed source values and association exclusions. Source water permittivity is temperature dependent; the legacy scalar 78.09 is not blanket authority for all temperatures.

The article models a neutral [emim][tcb] pseudo-component while discussing cation exchange (article lines 228 and 258). Separate charged [emim]+/[tcb]− and extracted-complex parameters are not supplied by that neutral tuple. Lithium's prior benchmark explicitly keeps this input limitation; it also assumes an overall feed ratio for subsystem S1/S6 because those tables do not supply one. Source-faithful quantitative parity cannot be obtained by inheriting that assumed ratio, adding trace salt to zero-salt rows, inventing charged species or transferring a Figiel parameter set. These are missing model/input definitions, not a newly demonstrated Engine numerical defect.

Full S3/S4 cation screening needs complete species parameters and basis; S8–S10 process rows require the same mechanism and feed definitions. They are not declared reproduced. The next scientific decision is whether the neutral pseudo-component model alone is the intended comparison, with complete declared feeds, or whether charged cation exchange is required. That choice belongs to the lead/application owner. No producer is added before it and independent readiness. Existing measured/model source rows support later comparisons but do not justify a live notebook or `current` index row now.
