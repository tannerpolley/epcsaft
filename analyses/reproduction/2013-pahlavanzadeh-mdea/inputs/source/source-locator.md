# Pahlavanzadeh and Fakouri Baygi 2013 source record

DOI: 10.1016/j.jct.2012.12.021. Zotero parent DUAIHQ7Y; PDF YAUNIZGX;
Markdown 44UN7WS8. The actual Markdown child ends in `Methyldiethanolamine Sol.md`.
Source identities, CSV hashes, locators and primary-table verification are retained
in source-identity.json and table-verification.json. Verified tables replace the
incomplete historical transcriptions; Git retains the original files.

The two figure CSVs and their calibration/QA images were copied unchanged from
accepted design evidence and committed before predictions. The four raster QA
files now live under `build/issue-736/source/`: `figure-1-overlay.png`,
`figure-4-overlay.png`, `figure-1-reading-strip.png` and `figure-4-reading-strips.png`.
Coordinates, calibration, image hashes and reading chronology remain here; historical
execution input hashes still describe the files present at those executions. No source readings
were changed after prediction. These are manually owned model coordinates,
not measured observations; the two-pixel rectangles describe reading resolution.
Full pages and primary PDFs remain in the archived build evidence.

The paper uses neutral molecular PC-SAFT plus separate ideal mole-fraction
chemistry. The native comparison uses raw Table 2 tuples with sigma-cubed
association; the paper prints d-cubed. Figure 4 has k_ij=0. The supplementary
molar masses come from nasrifar-2010-mdea/1 molecular identity only.
Kuranov and Kamps tables retain original temperatures and molality bases;
printed 10p values times 100000 give Pa. No later temperature correction is used.
Kamps matches the reported row count but its range differs from Table 6, so exact
aggregate-cohort identity is unresolved. Source experimental uncertainty is
retained separately from the half-print pressure criterion.

Eq. (14) prints negative bicarbonate and fails carbon conservation for reactions
II and III. Eq. (15), charge and total MDEA are conserved by all four reactions.
Table 5 prints positive C for autoprotolysis, conflicting with the negative-C
Nasrifar reference; neither correction is adopted. Reaction IV is fitted only
through 95 degrees Celsius; hotter reactive conditions extrapolate it.
The molecular feed mapping from ions is unspecified; #776 supplies ideal
chemistry capability, not evidence for a Pahlavanzadeh effective-water mapping.
Eq. (11) frozen scalars suffice for fixed-temperature pressure declarations;
excess enthalpy requires the thermal chain and a stated common pressure.

Figure 1 6C/DIPPR overlap, Figure 2 unkeyed curves, Figure 3 unidentified parity
states, Figure 5 overlapping same-style curves, Figure 6 close lines, Figure 7
MDEAH+/bicarbonate overlap and Figures 8-9 unkeyed model curves are not digitized:
ambiguous ownership. No primary Voutsas, Maham/Posey, Jou-Mather, Jakobsen,
Dalmolin/Valtz/Chapoy or Ma'mun/Lemoine row tables were recovered in this scope.
Figure 10 cites Kuranov in its caption but Ma'mun in the text. Figure 11 has
297/297.15/298 K alternatives and Table 6 reverses the apparent loading/pressure
ranges relative to the body; no column swap or guessed temperature is adopted.
