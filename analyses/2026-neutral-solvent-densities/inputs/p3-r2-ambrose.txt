We measured the vapour pressure from 5 to 200 kPa and the critical temperature
and critical pressure of 4-methylpentan-2-one    (isobutyl methyl ketone) by methods
described in the account of an investigation of aikanoic acids.(‘) Purchased material
was purified by distillation and the mole fraction of impurity in the sample used was
assessed by g.1.c. as 0.003. The values found for the critical temperature T, and
critical pressure per based on measurements made with two fillings of the
experimental tube, were (574.6 + 0.5) K and (3.270 + 0.005) MPa, respectively. The
compound is stable at its critical temperature and the critical pressure could be
measured directly by means of the piston pressure balance, but it was not possible
to define the temperature more closely than +0.5 K with the rapid heater, which it
was convenient to use for the work.
