# Source transcription provenance

Figure digitization: the worker's Fig. 13/15 point files were not retained. Fig. 15 phase labels and isotherm assignment were unresolved, so the lead redigitized it with `scripts/digitize_fig15.py` (filled symbols = liquid, open = vapor), written to `inputs/gv2006-fig15-acetone-pentane-digitized.csv`. Fig. 13 is not used.

All source PDFs were read from the owner's Zotero library in read-only mode. Their attached text was extracted with `pdftotext -layout`; values below were compared against those extracted table/caption lines. Figure points were extracted with the CSE Sources `digitize_xy_plot.py` helper from 300 dpi page renders. No source PDF was downloaded and no Zotero item was changed.

## Numeric and provenance limits

- No table value in the three TSVs was left unverifiable against its cited article text. GV2006 ketone temperature-range cells are blank in the source; the blanks are preserved. GV2006 does not print `n_mu` in Table 2, and Kleiner's parameter tables also do not include it.
- Figure-coordinate values are digitized estimates, not numbers printed in the paper. They are bounded by the per-coordinate uncertainties in the CSVs and checked by the overlays, not by text extraction.
- GV2006 Figures 16 and 18 do not state `k_ij` in their captions. The blanks are verified omissions; the Figure 16 de Villiers values are a separate later report and its p.175 sentence does not state a temperature.
- Esper et al.'s machine-readable parameter SI is not attached in Zotero, so no compound-level Esper values were transcribed. DOI/Crossref searches found no DOI for the Matvienko–YarymAgaev n-butane paper or the Nissema–Karvo DMSO/toluene paper; their citations are retained as printed in GV2006.
