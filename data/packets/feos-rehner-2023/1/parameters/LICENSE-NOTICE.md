# FeOs/Rehner data notice

This notice covers `parameters.json` in this folder, including the Esper pure
records required by the Rehner binary records. The packet retains the
CC BY-NC 4.0 use basis recorded in `../packet.toml` because of those Esper
dependencies: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>.

Attribution: T. Esper, G. Bauer, P. Rehner and J. Gross (2023),
“PCP-SAFT Parameters of Pure Substances Using Large Experimental Databases”,
DOI <https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.iecr.3c02255>, Supporting Information
<https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.iecr.3c02255.s001>.
P. Rehner, A. Bardow and J. Gross (2023), “Modeling Mixtures with PCP-SAFT:
Insights from Large-Scale Parametrization and Group-Contribution Method for
Binary Interaction Parameters”, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10765-023-03290-3>.
The Rehner publication separately records CC BY 4.0 attribution terms,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>, subject to its separately
credited third-party exclusions.

Source distribution: FeOs contributors, commit
`af84e3f9da2fac2d102d449cd07cbbb48237617b`,
`parameters/pcsaft/rehner2023_binary.json` and `parameters/pcsaft/esper2023.json`.
Changes are recorded in `../packet.toml` and `../provenance/source-origin.toml`:
decimal molar-mass conversion, explicit site topology and admission decisions,
association-volume basis conversion, and explicit mixing rules and zero strengths.
